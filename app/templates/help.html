Start a scenario

01Quick start

  1. Mission select: enter a callsign and unit, pick a scripted scenario or generate one from a seed, and press Enter.
  2. Scan the view and the radar. When you spot a contact, mark it by clicking it, or click its radar track.
  3. Classify it with 1 to 5, then respond with T R J I C.
  4. The session ends when every hostile is resolved or time runs out, or press Esc. The server scores it and shows the reason for every point.
  5. Review progress on the AAR dashboard.

02Threat markers

Every state is shown by shape and text as well as colour.

HOSTILEClassified as a recon or armed drone UNKNOWNMarked, not yet classified FRIENDLYClassified as a friendly aircraft BENIGNClassified as a bird, decoy or balloon

On the radar, an unmarked track is a cyan square with its track ID and size cue (S, M or L). The radar never shows what a contact is.

04Sensors and radar

  • Day EO: natural colour. Contacts are dark against the sky.
  • Night vision: green phosphor with grain. Faint contacts become visible at night.
  • Thermal: white-hot. Drones, birds and aircraft glow against a cold sky, and it sees further through haze.
  • Degradation (levels 0 to 3) adds noise and fog and cuts visible range from 3000 m down to 900 m. Lean on the radar.
  • Radar: a 30 rpm sweep, so tracks update every 2 seconds. Small drones and birds are only picked up within about 1.3 km. Low flyers hide in ground clutter, especially among buildings. A track forms after 2 hits in 3 sweeps and drops after 3 misses. Clicking a lone return with no target behind it is a false alarm.
